Filter syntax
who*
Procedures by name, * any run of characters
-name
Leaves a procedure out, which wins
d(rx)
Received, or tx sent
t(request|command)
Types of call, r, c and n for short
e(chunk|credit)
Envelopes by kind: request, result, failure, notification, chunk, credit, cancel

Words are parted by spaces or commas, and the values a | joins add up. It is bead:debug’s filter. Press / to reach it.
