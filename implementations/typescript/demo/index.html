Payload rules

A sign-up whose .proto holds protovalidate's rules, compiled by beadc: the server judges each request before its resolver, and answers one they refuse PAYLOAD_INVALID, each violation shown under its field.

      1 to 63 characters: a lower-case letter, then letters, digits or hyphens

        From 1 to 10

          Or a phone: exactly one of the two

            E.164: a +, then 7 to 15 digits

            The client judges the request by the same rules: a refusal fails at once, and nothing goes on the wire.

            Use a 256-byte client detail with Validate before sending to see a refusal whose list does not fit. Use the default detail.
