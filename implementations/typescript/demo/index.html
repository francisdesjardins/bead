              What the wire shows goes to the console too, with no reload:
              localStorage.setItem('bead:debug', '*'), or a filter such as
              d(rx), -token, and e(notification) for the envelopes. The
              Bead DevTools panel filters every view; -metrics, -stats leaves polling
              out of its Broker view where DATA joins its call. Uncertain captures stay visible.
            
                  
          
                  
        
                  

        
                  
          
                  
            
                  One contract, several packages
                  
            
                  
              Cut a blueprint into the outputs of beadc.yaml: each writes a
              server, a client and a gateway over its own
              selection of procedures, and each transport of its server takes the procedures it is
              given. Every choice comes from the blueprint; nothing is typed. What each class holds
              and what beadc generate would refuse are beadc's own code, run in this
              page.
            
                  
            
                  
              
                  
                
                
                
              
                  
              
                  
                Or drop here a bead-blueprint/1 file, which
                beadc blueprint writes; it stays in this browser. The page reads its
                services and their procedures alone: a blueprint's own checks are
                beadc check's.

                  What each class holds
                  
              
                  
              
                  
              
                  
                A filled dot: the class holds the procedure. The server class holds its resolvers,
                its notifiers and the requesters of its commands; the client class its requesters,
                its notifications and the resolvers of the commands it answers; the gateway its
                routes. A transport's column is what it takes: a request to any other procedure is
                answered SERVICE_UNKNOWN or PROCEDURE_UNKNOWN.

                  beadc.yaml's outputs
                  
            
                  
            
                  
              
              
            
                  
            
                  
            
                  
              Each folder and each transport's key is derived from what its package holds, never
              from its place in the list: removing a package renames no other. To name them
              otherwise, rename them in beadc.yaml; a key of
              server.transports is the member of
              SERVED_PROCEDURES (TypeScript) or ServedProcedures (C#) the
              application hands its transport. The file around these outputs keeps its
              version and its source: what depends on the disk, the
              .proto roots among it, stays beadc generate's.
